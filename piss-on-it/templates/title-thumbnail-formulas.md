# Title and Thumbnail Formulas

## Title formulas
Keep titles under about 60 characters, with the searchable keyword early.

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| `I Tested [Product] So You Don't Have To` | I Tested a $80 Smart Water Bottle So You Don't Have To |
| `[Product]: Worth $[Price]? (Honest Verdict)` | LED Face Masks: Worth $400? (Honest Verdict) |
| `Is [Trend] a Scam?` | Is Mouth Taping a Scam? |
| `I Bought [Number] [Category] From TikTok` | I Bought 10 TikTok Shop Gadgets. Ouch. |
| `$[Cheap] vs $[Expensive] [Product]` | $30 vs $300 Massage Gun |
| `The [Product] Lie` | The Smart Ring Lie |
| `Somebody Had to Piss on [Thing]` | Somebody Had to Piss on 75 Hard |

**Rules:**
- Don't put "Piss on It" in every title. The brand lives in the thumbnail and channel name, and search terms matter more in titles.
- Use crude words in the title rarely. They can hurt ad suitability.

## Thumbnail system

**Layout (the same on every video so the channel is recognizable):**
```
┌──────────────────────────────────┐
│ [PRODUCT, big, slightly tilted]  │
│                       [HOST FACE]│
│ [3–4 WORD TEXT, Anton, all caps] │
│                   [VERDICT STAMP]│  ← red/yellow, rotated -8°
│ POI 💧 (small corner badge)      │
└──────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Background:** solid Stream Yellow `#FFD60A` or Porcelain Black `#111111`.
- **Text:** 4 words max, and never the same words as the title.
  - Examples: "$80 FOR WATER?", "IT LIED", "I'M NOT OK", "WAIT… IT'S GOOD?"
- **Verdict stamp:** you can leave it blurred or covered with "???" to make people curious, or show it boldly for Flush It episodes.
- **Host expression:** disgust, disbelief, or a smug side-eye. Keep it consistent.
- **Never** show actual liquid on the product. A cartoon yellow droplet icon is enough.

## A/B testing
Use YouTube's "Test & Compare" on every long video: change one thing per test (the text, the expression, or whether the verdict is visible).
