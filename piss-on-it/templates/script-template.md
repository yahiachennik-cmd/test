## 0:00–0:20 | Cold open / hook
- Start with the most absurd claim, price, or clip. No intro, no "hey guys".
- End the hook with the stakes: *"Today's victim: ___. Let's see if it deserves the stream."*
- 🎬 *Zipper sting and logo, under 2 seconds.*

## 0:20–1:30 | The promise
- What does it claim? Show its own ad or website.
- Who's selling it, and how hyped is it? (views, sales numbers, celebrity endorsements)
- Joke beat: one punchline about the marketing language.

## 1:30–6:00 | The test
- What I actually did with it, and for how long.
- Break it into 2–3 mini-segments, each with a payoff.
- Comparison: a cheap alternative or the free alternative ("…or just drink water").
- Keep a re-hook every 60–90 seconds: a question, a reveal, a "but wait".

## 6:00–8:30 | The fine print
- Hidden costs (subscriptions, refills, an app paywall).
- What the science or experts actually say, with sources.
- Reviews the brand doesn't show you.

## 8:30–9:30 | Verdict
- *"Alright. Zipper down."*
- 💦 **PISS ON IT** / 🧻 **FLUSH IT** / 🏆 **HOLD IT**
- Bladder Meter: __ / 10
- One-sentence summary that a viewer could quote in the comments.

## 9:30–10:00 | Outro
- *"What should I piss on next? Comments."*
- End screen: the best-performing previous video and a subscribe button.
- *"Stay hydrated. Stay honest. Piss on it."*
