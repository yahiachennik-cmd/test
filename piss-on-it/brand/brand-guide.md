# Brand Guide: Piss on It

## Name

**Piss on It** ("piss" as in piss).

- Always write it **Piss on It**: capital P, capital I, lowercase "on".
- Short form: **POI**. Use it in hashtags and in the thumbnail corner badge.
- Never "Peace on It" and never "Pee on It". It is **Piss** (P-I-S-S).

## Taglines (pick one, rotate the others)

1. **"If it's overhyped, we piss on it."** (primary)
2. "Honest reviews. Zero bladder control."
3. "Somebody had to."
4. "Hype goes in. The truth comes out."

## Positioning

Honest, funny takedowns of overhyped stuff, for people who are sick of sponsored "this changed my life" content.

**We are:** the funny friend who actually reads the fine print.
**We are not:** a rage channel, a hate channel, or gross-out shock content.

## Voice and tone

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Blunt, quick, confident | Mean to regular people |
| Self-aware toilet humor (puns, sound effects) | Show actual bodily fluids. Keep it implied and cartoonish |
| Back every roast with a real fact, test, or price | Make claims you can't back up (defamation risk) |
| Punch up: brands, gurus, trends, billion-dollar companies | Punch down: small creators, kids, private individuals |
| Give credit when something is actually good ("Hold It") | Fake outrage for clicks |

**Recurring lines:**
- Open: *"Today's victim: ___. Let's see if it deserves the stream."*
- Before the verdict: *"Alright. Zipper down."*
- Sign-off: *"Stay hydrated. Stay honest. Piss on it."*

## Verdict system

| Verdict | Meaning | On-screen graphic |
|---|---|---|
| 💦 **PISS ON IT** | Overhyped or overpriced. Skip it. | Yellow droplet stamp |
| 🧻 **FLUSH IT** | Actively bad, a scam, or harmful | Toilet-flush swirl animation |
| 🏆 **HOLD IT** | Actually good. Respect earned. | Gold "HELD" seal |

Optional sub-score: the **Bladder Meter** (0–10). 10 means "I need to go immediately".

Thumbnails: yellow or black backgrounds, with red reserved for the verdict stamp.

## Typography

- **Headlines and thumbnails:** *Anton* or *Bebas Neue* (free on Google Fonts), all caps.
- **Body and captions:** *Inter*.
- **Verdict stamps:** *Anton*, slightly rotated (−8°), with a rough edge.

## Logo

- The mark is a **yellow droplet with a cheeky face**, and the wordmark is "PISS ON IT".
- Profile picture: the droplet only, on black.
- Starter files: `logo.svg` and `banner.svg` in this folder.

## Sound identity

- Intro sting: a zipper sound, then a short beat drop (under 2 seconds).
- Verdict: a stream sound effect that cuts off on a record scratch for "Hold It".
- Transitions: a toilet flush whoosh, used sparingly.
